1. Introduction

RatnoX Games respects your privacy. This policy explains how COLOR POUR handles information when you use our mobile game.

2. Information We Collect

COLOR POUR does not require account creation, registration or login.

Game progress, coins, preferences and username, where applicable, may be stored locally on your device. The current version does not use a cloud-based game-progress account system.

COLOR POUR uses Google AdMob to display advertisements, including banner, interstitial and rewarded advertisements where enabled.

Google Mobile Ads SDK may process information such as IP address, advertising identifiers, device information, advertisement interactions and diagnostic information. This information may be used for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention and service improvement.

Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. How Information Is Used

  • To save and maintain local game progress.
  • To display advertisements.
  • To measure advertising performance.
  • To help prevent advertising fraud.
  • To maintain and improve app functionality.

5. Information Sharing

We do not sell users' personal information. Google and its service providers may process information through the advertising SDK to provide advertising, analytics and security services.

6. Data Storage and Security

Game progress and preferences are stored locally on your device. Advertising-related information may be processed by Google according to its privacy and security practices.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Local game information generally remains on your device until you clear the app's storage, uninstall the app or otherwise delete it. Information processed by Google is subject to Google's applicable retention policies.

For privacy questions, contact ratnoxofficial@gmail.com.

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Advertising and age-related settings are configured according to the app's declared audience and applicable requirements.
