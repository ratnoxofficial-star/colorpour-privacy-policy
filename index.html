1. Introduction

COLOR POUR is a mobile color-sorting puzzle game distributed for Android under the RatnoX Games brand. This Privacy Policy explains, in accessible terms, what information may be stored or processed when you use the game, including processing by third-party components integrated into a particular release. It also explains how to make a privacy inquiry.

Information handling can vary with the installed version, device settings, region, advertising choices and the services enabled in that release. This policy should be read alongside the Terms & Conditions.
